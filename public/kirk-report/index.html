The homepage now shows the actual wind and temperature at the Golden Gate right now, pulled free of charge from the National Weather Service, plus tonight's computed sunset time — and when the sunset sail would cast off. Part two

What the machines see — and why that now matters more than anything

A growing share of your future customers will never see a list of websites at all. They'll ask an AI a question, and the AI will answer with one or two recommended businesses. This section explains how we made sure that answer is you.

People have changed how they search

900Mpeople use ChatGPT every weekOpenAI, Feb 2026
2B+people see Google's AI-written answers monthlyGoogle, 2025
58%of U.S. adults under 30 use ChatGPTPew Research, 2025

When someone asks, "What's the best private sailing charter in San Francisco?", ChatGPT, Claude, and Google's AI don't guess. They read websites — and they can only recommend businesses whose sites they can read and verify. Both OpenAI and Anthropic publish documentation on the automated readers ("crawlers") that decide which businesses appear in AI answers.3 Google says the same thing in its own guidance: there's no magic trick — AI visibility comes from crawlable pages, clear content, and structured data.4

We ran the test ourselves

We asked Claude — the AI assistant, on a normal phone — where to go sailing in San Francisco. It recommended two of your competitors by name: Adventure Cat and SF Bay Adventures, "out of Sausalito for smaller-group sails."

Read that twice. The AI recommended someone else for small-group sails out of Sausalito — which is exactly what you are, with better reviews than either of them (5.0 across three platforms; #1 of 42 things to do in Sausalito).

That's not the AI being unfair. It's the AI working with what it can read — and the old site gives it almost nothing to read. Every change in this section exists to fix precisely this: the structured business data, the plain-text prices, the machine-readable FAQ. The goal is simple: the next time someone asks that question, the answer includes Captain Kirk's.

Adobe tracked millions of visits to U.S. travel sites: traffic arriving from AI assistants has grown more than twenty-fold since late 2024, and those visitors browse longer, bounce far less, and arrive ready to decide.5 These are exactly the customers a five-star business wants — people who asked for "the best" and got sent to you.

What we actually did about it

  • A machine-readable business card on every page. Hidden in the code (the industry term is "structured data" or "schema"), every page now carries a precise, standardized description of your business: name, phone, address, hours, prices, boats, the 2002 founding date, your Cemetery & Funeral Bureau license number. Humans never see it; every AI and search engine does.
  • Your FAQ is now machine-answerable. All 18 questions and answers are marked up in the format Google and AI assistants ingest directly — so "can I bring my dog on a sailing charter in SF?" has one obvious source: you. (Answer aboard: yes, and you carry dog life jackets.)
  • Prices, durations, and capacities live in plain text. Sounds obvious — but two of your four main competitors publish no consumer prices at all, and a third contradicts itself between pages. An AI asked "how much does a private sail cost in San Francisco" can quote your site cleanly. Theirs, it can't.
  • Google's own case studies show pages with structured data earning 25–82% more clicks from the same search position.6

Housekeeping that protects your Google ranking

  • Your address appears three different ways across the internet (150, 180, and 310 Harbor Dr). Google reads that inconsistency as unreliability. The new site states one canonical address per boat, and we'll align your Google Business Profile at handoff.
  • Your current site has a broken secure-connection setup: typing "sfbaysail.com" routes visitors through an insecure hop before landing. Chrome has flagged such connections as "Not secure" since 20187 — and nearly half of users say they won't enter personal details on a site flagged that way.8 The new site is fully secure from the first request, and we'll fix the domain routing at handoff.
What this earns you: when the fastest-growing discovery channel in travel recommends "a private sailing charter in San Francisco," yours is the business it can actually read, verify, and quote — while every competitor's site gives it nothing to work with.
