01  ·  What I ran into

Four problems, each costing bookings.

Earlier this summer I started looking at Clear Lake rentals for me and a few friends. These are the things that came up when I tried to read your site and book on my phone.

Problem 01

The pricing table falls apart on a phone.

Open the site on a phone, or inside an in-app browser like ChatGPT, Claude, or Instagram, and the rental pricing table runs off the screen and overlaps the page header. It's nearly unreadable. Most people planning a lake day are on their phone, and a growing share of younger customers like myself now open businesses from inside an app rather than Safari or Chrome.

Most people don't try to scroll horizontally to read a pricing table. They give up and check the next option.

Problem 02

I had to call to find out what a tube and wakeboard cost.

The add-on pricing isn't visible on the main page or any of the main FareHarbor rental pages. I had to pick up the phone and ask. Most people won't, and those bookings quietly leave.

If a customer can see the add-on price, they add it. If they can't, most won't pick up the phone to ask.

Problem 03

Sections are cut off and spacing is broken.

Reviews get clipped mid-sentence. Headers crowd the images. The gallery has large empty gaps.

These details matter most exactly when a customer is about to commit. Right now they're working against you.

Problem 04

You're nearly invisible in AI search for "things to do."

More and more people are discovering local businesses through AI search now, not Google. I tested how the latest models answer travel questions. For best boat rentals on Clear Lake, you come up on top. You've won that. But for things to do in Lakeport, the question someone asks before they've decided to rent a boat, you're only mentioned some of the time, or not at all, compared to other specific companies. That's the fastest-growing way millennials and Gen Z plan trips. Right now it's sending them elsewhere.

When the trip planning happens inside ChatGPT or Claude, the businesses that get named are the ones who win the day.

Claude's "Things to do in Lakeport?" answer this week. Library Park, Mt. Konocti, and a competitor's boat rental show up. Disney's does not.

None of this is a knock on the business. It's a website that was built once and left alone while the business grew up around it. That's normal. It's also very fixable.

02  ·  An optional add-on

A small live lake conditions panel.

An idea you can take or leave. A simple readout of Clear Lake conditions, right on the rentals page, refreshed automatically. It answers the question every customer has before they commit: what's it going to be like out there?

Fishing trips care about glassy water. Families care about temperature and rain. Wake sports care about wind. A single small panel answers all of them and shows the calmest stretch of the day, so a customer on the fence books today instead of leaving to check the weather and forgetting to come back. If the weather service is briefly unreachable, the widget falls back to the last good reading rather than a broken box.

03  ·  What I'll do

Seven fixes, and what each one earns you.

Every one of these is a permanent change. We do it once, and it keeps working for you, every season, for every future customer.

  1. Fix the pricing display so it renders cleanly on every screen and in-app browser.
    Customers can actually read your prices and book on their phone, where most of them are.
  2. Surface tube, wakeboard, and rental pricing as plain readable text.
    Kills the "how much is a tube?" phone calls and the silent drop-offs.
  3. Fix spacing, cut-off sections, and display errors.
    The site finally looks as trustworthy as your reviews say you are.
  4. Add structured data so Google and AI assistants can read your prices and fleet.
    You show up in search results and AI answers with real info, not a blank.
  5. Expand the FAQ from the questions people are actually asking on Reddit, Google, and elsewhere.
    Answers the hesitations that stop a booking, before anyone has to call.
  6. Speed and load-time pass on every page.
    Faster pages keep more visitors and rank better.
  7. Conversion tracking on the booking flow.
    So we can both see, in real numbers, how many bookings these changes drive.

04  ·  What this is worth

Doing the math, using your own numbers.

A single 4-hour ski boat rental on your site is $545. A pontoon day runs into the four figures with add-ons. If these fixes bring in even one or two extra rentals, they've already paid for themselves, many times over.

It won't just be one or two extra rentals. A site that's readable on phones, shows its prices, and turns up in AI search doesn't help you a single time. It helps you with every customer who finds you, every weekend, every season from now on.

The right time to fix a leaking funnel is right now, when we're in the middle of your highest-revenue months.
