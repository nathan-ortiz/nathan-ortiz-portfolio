OCCASIONS

Ages 4 and up. We mean it.

Kids ride tandem with an adult in wide, stable kayaks, from the most protected launch on the coast. Reviewers have brought 3 year olds and 74 year olds on the same trip.

For the grownups

Built for first timers, sized for small ones

The launch cove at Van Damme is calm on almost every day the tours run, the kayaks are the wide kind you would have to work hard to tip, and the guides have spent 25 years putting nervous first timers at ease. Kids ride in the front seat of a tandem with a parent behind them. The seals do the entertaining. If a young paddler runs out of steam, the group paces to the slowest boat, always.

  • Ages 4 and up, kids tandem with an adult
  • Kids 12 and under are $40, adults $80
  • Life vests in every size, fitted on the beach
  • 90 minutes, the right length before anyone melts down
  • Grandparents welcome, no experience needed
What to bring. Layers, sandals or water shoes, sunscreen, and a dry change of clothes in the car. The full list lives on Plan Your Trip.
