THE PEOPLE

Twenty-five years reading this coast.

Kayak Mendocino is what happens when a professional surfer gets a marine science degree and never leaves the water.

The founder

Craig Comen

Craig surfed professionally from 1979 to 1990, then traded contests for a marine science degree from College of the Redwoods and a kayak. He has guided this specific stretch of coast since 1997, first with Lost Coast Kayaking and then under his own flag as Kayak Mendocino. Ask him what lives in the tidepool on the north wall of the third cave and settle in. He still guides most days, still reads the swell each morning, and still thinks the best classroom on earth has kelp in it. His mission, in his words, is to open up others' eyes, hearts and minds to the world's oceans.

  • Official kayak tour concessionaire, Van Damme State Park
  • Guiding here since 1997, surfing a decade before that
  • Marine science background, naturalist to the bone
  • Named in more reviews than we can count, usually next to the word "knowledgeable"

The crew

Small crew, deep water knowledge

You will usually meet Craig or Mark on the beach. Mark has guided here for years and reviewers keep using the same words for him: punctual, patient, encyclopedic about the birds. Guides here learn the caves before they lead them, and every trip runs at the pace of its slowest paddler. That is policy, not kindness.
