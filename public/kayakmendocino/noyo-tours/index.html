Tours · Noyo Harbor, Fort Bragg

The river that smells like salt.

A gentle 90 minute paddle through a working harbor and a protected estuary on the Fort Bragg side of the coast. $80 adults, $40 kids 12 and under.

The experience

Harbor out, estuary back

The Noyo is the calmest water we run. You launch inside the harbor, paddle past the fishing fleet unloading the day's catch, and slip under the Highway 1 bridge into the quiet upper river, where the redwood hills come down to the banks. Seals and sea lions work the harbor mouth. Herons and osprey work the flats. When the ocean is too rough for the caves, this trip is usually still perfect, which makes it the right call for travelers who need certainty on a fixed date.

  • Calmest option, ideal first paddle
  • Runs in most weather, all season
  • Same wildlife density, different cast: sea lions, otters, osprey
  • Ages 4 and up, gear included

When the caves call

If you came to Mendocino for the sea caves, book the Sea Cave Tour at Van Damme first. The Noyo is what we recommend for a second day on the water, for very young paddlers, or for days when the swell keeps the caves closed.

Prefer to stay dry?

The quiet boat option

Our partners at GOAL, a local nonprofit, run a one hour Noyo River and Bay tour aboard a quiet electric boat. Same harbor, same seals, no paddle. $50 adults, $25 kids 12 and under. Booked through us by phone, run by GOAL. It is a separate operation and we want you to know that up front, we just think it's great.
