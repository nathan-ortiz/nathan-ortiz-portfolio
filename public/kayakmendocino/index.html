Who you'll meet out there

The locals are friendly

Harbor seals haul out on the rocks at the cave mouths and often slide in to follow the kayaks. Cormorants and murres work the cliffs. At low tide the cave walls turn into tidepools you can read like a field guide: sea stars, urchins, anemones, sea palms.

  • Harbor seals, most trips, year round
  • Sea lions and river otters in the Noyo estuary
  • Gray whales offshore, November through April
  • Sea stars, urchins, and anemones at low tide

Your guide

Twenty-five years reading this coast

Craig Comen surfed professionally for a decade, took a degree in marine science, and has been guiding kayaks on the Mendocino coast since 1997. He reads the swell before you launch, picks the caves the ocean is offering that day, and tells you what you're looking at while you float through it. Kayak Mendocino is the official kayak tour concessionaire of Van Damme State Park.
