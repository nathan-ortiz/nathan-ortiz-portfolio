The experience

Ninety minutes, start to wonder

You meet the white van with our logo in the beach parking lot at Van Damme State Park. Your guide fits you with a life vest and a paddle, walks you through the basics on the sand, and launches you from the most protected cove on this stretch of coast. Ten minutes of easy paddling later you are at the cave mouths. Then your guide reads the swell, picks the passages the ocean is offering that day, and leads you through them one kayak at a time. Sunlight comes through the openings and moves on the water. Harbor seals watch from the rocks and often slide in to follow. Nobody rushes you.

  • All gear included: stable tandem kayaks, life vests, paddles
  • Guides trained in these specific caves, not just kayaking
  • Kids 4 and up ride tandem with an adult
  • Protected launch, calm even on foggy days

The honest part

Will the caves be open?

The ocean decides, and we want you to know that before you book. Tides and swell change which caves are safe to enter on any given day. Most days the answer is plenty. Some days the answer is fewer, and your guide will trade caves for sea stacks, kelp forests, and the seal haul-outs along the bluffs. If conditions are truly unsafe we will tell you before you drive out, not after you arrive. Read more on the Plan Your Trip page.

From the water

What it looks like in there

Keep an eye out for

Harbor seals. Haul out at the cave mouths year round and often trail the kayaks. The locals reviewers write home about.

Sea stars and urchins. At low tide the cave walls turn into tidepools. Orange and purple stars, green anemones, spiny urchins.

Common murres. Penguin-looking seabirds that raft up under the cliffs and dive right beside the boats.
