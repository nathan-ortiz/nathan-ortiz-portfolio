The gray whales commute past our front door.

Twenty thousand gray whales migrate along this coast twice each winter, Alaska to Baja and back. Some of them pass close enough to hear.

The season

Winter is a secret around here

Summer gets the crowds, but locals know the Mendocino coast does its best work in winter. The air is clearest after rain, the bluffs go green, and from December through April the gray whale migration streams past the headlands. On calm winter days we run the Sea Cave Tour as usual, and a whale spout offshore is a regular bonus. When the swell is up we will tell you straight and point you to the best land lookouts instead, the headlands at Mendocino and the tower at Point Cabrillo.

  • Southbound migration December to February, northbound with calves February to April
  • Winter tours run on calm days, always weather called
  • Whale sightings from kayaks are a bonus, never a promise
  • Book winter dates by phone so we can talk conditions first: 707-813-7117
An honest note. Nobody can guarantee a whale from a kayak, and anyone who does is selling something. What we can promise is the coast in its winter coat, empty caves, and a guide watching the horizon with you.
