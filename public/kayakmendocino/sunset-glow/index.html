The experience

Golden hour, then some

Evening on the Noyo is a different river. The fishing fleet is in, the wind lies down, and the water goes to glass. You paddle toward the harbor mouth as the sun drops, watch the sky do its show over the Pacific, and drift back under the bridge in the afterglow. We light the kayaks with soft LEDs for the return, which the kids treat as the second show. Bring a warm layer, the temperature drops with the sun.

  • Launches around 7:30pm in season
  • Calm protected water, beginner friendly
  • LED lit kayaks for the twilight return
  • The couples favorite, and honeymooners should see the Couples page

The glow is the sunset and our lights, not bioluminescence. On this coast the ocean saves that trick for rare warm nights, and we would rather promise you the sky.
