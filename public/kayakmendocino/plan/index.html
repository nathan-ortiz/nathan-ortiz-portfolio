Will the caves be open?

How tides and swell decide your day, what to wear, where to park, and everything else people call to ask. Five minutes here saves you a phone call, though we do like the calls.

Conditions

The ocean writes the itinerary

Every morning your guide reads the tide, the swell height, and the wind before deciding which caves are safe to enter. Most summer days the answer is most of them. Low swell means deeper passages, high swell means the outer arches and the wildlife route instead. This is not fine print, it is the nature of putting small boats inside a moving ocean, and it is why every tour is guided. If the day is truly unpaddleable we will call you before you drive out and rebook or refund you, your choice.

Want to play forecaster? The National Weather Service marine forecast for our water is at weather.gov, and swell under 6 feet is generally a good sign for caves. Your guide makes the real call.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need experience?
No. Most guests have never kayaked before. Your guide teaches you everything you need on the beach before you launch.
What should I wear?
Layers you can move in, plus shorts or pants that dry quickly and a long sleeve top. On your feet, go barefoot or wear sandals or water shoes. We provide all the necessary kayaking gear.
How do I pay?
You pay at the time of your trip, not in advance. We accept credit cards, PayPal, Venmo, and cash, though cash is preferred.
What ages can come?
Ages 4 and up. Kids ride tandem with an adult. Kids 12 and under are $40.
Where do we meet?
Van Damme State Park beach parking lot, Little River, on Highway 1 three miles south of Mendocino village. Look for the white van with our logo.
What if the weather looks bad?
Fog is normal here and the cove is protected, tours run happily in it. If conditions are unsafe we call you first, rebook or refund.
Will we see seals?
Almost always, they live here. Whales are a winter bonus, never a promise.
Can I bring a camera or phone?
Yes, at your own risk. A leash or dry bag is smart, and your guide will shoot a few photos at the best spots if you ask.
