Why this works

A tandem kayak is a team sport

You share a boat, you find a rhythm, and ten minutes later you are floating in a sea cave with the light moving on the water and nobody else in the world. Most couples book the Sea Cave Tour in the morning and dinner in Mendocino village after. The Sunset Glow tour is the evening version, and the one we quietly recommend for proposals: the light does half the work, and your guide can hang back with a camera at the right moment. Just tell us when you book.

  • Proposals: tell us, we have done this before, the camera is free
  • Anniversaries and honeymoons: morning caves, village dinner, no notes
  • Private tours available for just the two of you, see Groups & Private
  • Gift certificates: see Gift a Tour
