Your people, your boat,
and the whole Bay to yourselves.

Every charter is your group and our crew — never strangers. And our boats aren't floating patios; they're real sailing yachts, including an ocean racer that's run to Hawaii five times. Heel into the wind with a drink in hand, take the helm yourself, or just watch the city glide by. You choose.

Our crews have logged more than 200,000 ocean miles. Around here, the aim for every trip is the same: best day ever.

The crew

Sail with people the reviews mention by name.

Captain Kirk Miller left a twenty-year engineering career (and a Stanford master's degree) to sail full time. His captains have delivered yachts across the Atlantic, raced to national championships, and soloed from Hawaii to San Francisco. All hold Coast Guard licenses. Several will teach you to sail, free, on any trip.
