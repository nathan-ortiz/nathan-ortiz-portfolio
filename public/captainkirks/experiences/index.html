Sailing experiences

Where do you want to point the bow?

Every charter here is private — your group, your boat, your crew. What follows are starting points, not scripts. Tell your captain what you're after and any trip can be adjusted, mid-sail if you want.

4 hours · most popular

The Classic Four-Hour

The trip most people picture when they picture sailing San Francisco. Out under the Golden Gate Bridge, then a turn back along the full waterfront — Crissy Field, Aquatic Park, Pier 39, the Ferry Building, and Oracle Park if you're here for baseball. Past Alcatraz and Angel Island on the way home. Four hours is enough time to actually settle in.

Casts off ~2.5 hours before sunset

The Sunset Sail

Out toward the main bay as the light turns gold, then you watch the sun go down under the Golden Gate and sail home in the afterglow. Bring good food and drink — we mean that as encouragement, not a suggestion. In the colder months we push the departure earlier to catch the same light.

3 hours

The Three-Hour

Same loop as the Classic, run on a tighter schedule. It's the right call if you've got small children aboard, elders who tire before the rest of the group, or just a tight timeline for the day. Nobody feels shortchanged — the Bay does a lot in three hours.

1–6 guests · Bay Wolf

Ocean Sailing

For people who want real swell under the hull, not just Bay chop. We take Bay Wolf — a Santa Cruz 50 that's raced to Hawaii five times — out past Point Bonita and Muir Beach into the open Pacific. This is the trip for sailors who already know what they're after.

A few more ways out

Trips with a destination.

Harbor Porpoise Trips

seasonal · no extra charge

Harbor porpoises came back to the Bay after 60 years away, and they're not shy. On a good day they'll surface close enough that you can hear them exhale — that quick, unmistakable whoosh.

Sam's Anchor Café Dock-and-Dine

sail · lunch · sail home

Sail across to Tiburon, tie up at the dock, and have lunch at Sam's — a waterfront institution since 1920. Then back on the boat and home. Simple, and one of our most requested trips.

Angel Island Barbecue

10–40 people

Sail to Angel Island, where our chefs cook kabobs right on the beach. Hike if you're up for it, then sail back full and a little sunburned. For bigger groups, this is a favorite for corporate days.

Sailing lessons

Learn to sail, on any trip you already booked.

Lessons are free on any charter — you're not paying extra to learn, you're just asking. Our instructor-captains are ASA-certified and will tailor the lesson to wherever you're starting from, complete beginner to someone brushing up before buying their own boat. Four hours is the sweet spot for a real lesson — enough time to leave the dock, work through the fundamentals, and actually feel it click. And there's no better teaching boat than an ex-ocean-racer that knows how to move.
