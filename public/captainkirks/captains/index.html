The crew

Licensed. Seasoned. Named in the reviews.

Every captain here holds a Coast Guard license, and most carry six figures of ocean miles. Guests mention them by name in reviews, over and over.

Between them, our crew has logged more than 200,000 ocean miles together.

Kirk Miller

100-ton USCG Master · Sailing 40+ years

Kirk is the founder. He spent twenty years as a civil engineer, with a master's in the field from Stanford, before he left the office for the water full time and started the company in 2002. He'd already spent about twenty years teaching at Bay Area sailing schools by then, and he's an ASA-certified instructor, so if you actually want to learn to sail, he's the one to ask.

His own logbook includes the 3,000-mile crossing from the Galapagos to French Polynesia, 22 days at sea, and an 8-month, 5,000-mile expedition down the Pacific coast and into the Sea of Cortez.

Mark Steven Pomianowski

100-ton Master since 1983 · Sailing 40+ years

Mark grew up in an East Coast sailing family, and his first job on the water was as a commercial fisherman off Georges Banks. He came up through Chapmans School of Seamanship in '82, then delivered a 115-foot motor yacht across the Atlantic to Gibraltar and stayed on as first officer touring the Mediterranean. He spent years teaching and running charters around Martinique, St. Lucia, and the Yucatán before he found his way to the Bay.

He's also a licensed acupuncturist. With us since 2012.

Efe Akmen

100-ton Master · Sailing 25+ years

Efe started sailing in Turkey at age ten. He still races (J/111s and J/105s on the Bay) when he's not out on a charter. With us since 2014.
