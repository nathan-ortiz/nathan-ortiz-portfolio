Corporate & team building

The offsite they'll actually talk about.

No conference room. No trust falls. Just a real sailing yacht, your team working the winches (or not, plenty of people are happy to hold a drink and watch the city go by), and the Golden Gate overhead the whole time.

Pick your format

Three ways to get a team on the water.

One Boat

Up to 20 guests

One yacht, your whole group aboard. The default for offsites, client days, and celebrations: everyone in the same place, no logistics headache.

The Regatta

Bigger teams · multiple yachts

Split a bigger team across several boats and let them race each other across the Bay. Nothing builds a team like beating the group from finance. Bragging rights are forever.

Angel Island Day

10-40 people · full day

Sail over, hike a while, run a scavenger hunt (45 to 90 minutes), then sit down to a chef-cooked beachside barbecue: fresh chicken, beef, and vegetable kabobs. Sail home slow, sun doing the work.
