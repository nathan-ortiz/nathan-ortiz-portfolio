Memorial sails

A quiet goodbye, done right.

Since 2002, families have come to us to say goodbye on the water, near the Golden Gate, off Angel Island, or wherever felt right to the person they loved.

How it works

Simple, and unhurried.

  • Mornings are usually best. Around 10 AM, the wind is light and the water gentle.
  • Your group, our crew. No one else aboard.
  • The ceremony moves at your pace. We handle the boat, the location, and all of the paperwork.
  • We're licensed by the California Cemetery and Funeral Bureau (#CRD878).
