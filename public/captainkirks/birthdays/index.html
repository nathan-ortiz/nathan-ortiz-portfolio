Birthdays

The party where nobody checks their phone.

Your day, your rules

Make it yours.

  • Bring your own cake, food, and drinks — no corkage fee. Beer, white wine, and champagne are all fair game (no red wine, no hard liquor), one drink per person per hour. Friends don't let friends fall off of sailboats.
  • Take the helm on your own birthday — plenty of guests do.
  • Dogs are welcome aboard. We provide the dog life jackets.
  • Add a stop: a dock-and-dine lunch at Sam's in Tiburon, or a beach barbecue on Angel Island for bigger groups. See more in Experiences.
  • There's no age limit on a good day — a 90-year-old grandmother who took the helm is in our logbook.
