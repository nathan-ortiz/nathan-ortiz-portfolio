Pricing

Whole-boat prices. No surprises.

Every private charter is priced for the whole boat, not per person — your group is the only group aboard. Three-hour minimum on private trips. Tax included; crew gratuity (about 20%, like a restaurant) isn't.

The fine print

What's included, and good to know.

What's included

  • USCG-licensed captain and crew
  • Bring your own food and drink, no corkage fee (beer, white wine, champagne — no red wine, no hard liquor)
  • Depart Sausalito free, or Pier 40 San Francisco for a $350 docking fee

Good to know

  • 50% deposit reserves your date
  • Cards, debit, or cash
  • Rain means reschedule or full refund
  • Cancellations: more than a week out, 15%; inside a week, 50%; inside 24 hours, 100% unless we can rebook the slot
