The fleet

Three boats. One raced to Hawaii five times.

All three are professionally maintained sailing yachts that really sail, not floating patios. Which one is right for you depends on your group: how many, what kind of day, how much wind you're after. Tell us what you've got in mind and we'll match you to a boat in one phone call.

The racer

Bay Wolf (Santa Cruz 50)

Bay Wolf isn't dressed up as a racer. She is one. Five runs to Hawaii, in both the Transpac and the Pacific Cup. She was chartered by the first all-women's Transpac team, and she shows up in Disney's 100 Years of the Transpac. She's been part of the family since 2008, carrying a new Ballenger mast, a hard dodger, and Furuno radar.

None of that makes her intimidating. Our favorite line about her, and it's true: ten-year-old kids and ninety-year-old sailors sail her with ease.

The comfortable classic

Osprey (Gulfstar 50) ketch

Osprey trades a little speed for a lot of comfort. Her center cockpit and enclosed salon seat a dozen around a table, refrigerator included, the right call for a cool evening, grandparents aboard, or a long, unhurried lunch on the water. She went through a full refit in 2011-12 and is berthed at Clipper Yacht Harbor in Sausalito.

The intimate one

Mahalo (Freedom 35)

Mahalo is a 1995 Dave Pedrick design built around one idea: keep it simple. Her freestanding 54-ft carbon-fiber mast carries one big sail: no headsail to tack, no forestay in the way. That makes her wonderfully easy to sail, and just as easy to learn on. She joined the fleet in January 2018 after a full refit, and she sails from Sausalito only.

A note on capacity

The two 50-footers are USCG-certified to carry 24 people. We sail them with up to 20 on weekdays and 16 on weekends. Comfort over capacity, always.
